What is an AI SDR agent?

An AI SDR agent is software that does the work of a sales development rep (SDR), the junior seller who books first meetings for the closers. The job has seven steps: find, research, write, send, follow up, handle replies, book. Full agents try all seven. Copilots research and draft, and a person clicks send.

Autopilot means the agent sends without asking. Copilot means it drafts and you approve. Start in copilot. Switch when the drafts stop needing edits. For agents that run ads and content too, see Best AI Agents for Marketing in 2026.

What can an AI SDR replace, and what can't it?

An AI SDR replaces the repeatable work: research, drafts, follow-ups and fast replies. It does not replace the person who decides who to sell to, what to offer and how to run a live call. Adoption is no longer the question: Salesforce (a vendor) says nine in 10 sales teams use agents or expect to within two years.

What it replaces

  • Research and list building. Apollo sells access to 240M contacts. 11x pulls from 50+ data sources. No human reads that much.
  • Follow-ups. 42% of cold email replies come after the first email (Instantly, vendor, 2026). People skip step four. Agents do not.
  • Speed to lead. Firms that tried to contact a lead within an hour were nearly 7× as likely to qualify it as firms that waited one more hour, and over 60× as likely as firms that waited a day (HBR, 2011). In the same research, 23% of 2,241 US companies never answered a test lead. The average reply took 42 hours. An inbound agent answers in minutes, at 3 a.m. too.

What it can't replace

  • The market and the offer. An agent sends faster. It does not make a weak offer strong. As Gartner's Dan Gottlieb put it: "If those systems are fragmented, the agents will scale the fragmentation."
  • Trust. On LinkedIn, 28.5% of connection requests get accepted. After that, messages get 10.4% replies, against 3.0% for notes sent with the request (Expandi, vendor, 13.2M requests, May 2025 – April 2026). Connect first, pitch second.
  • Live calls. Even Artisan splits the job: Ava emails, your reps dial. Amplemarket keeps reps in control of every send.
  • Ownership of the number. Gartner predicts agents will outnumber sellers 10 to 1 by 2028, yet fewer than 40% of sellers will say agents improved their productivity (July 2026, 210 sales leaders surveyed).

So, will AI replace SDRs? It replaces SDR tasks. Most teams buy a tool and expect it to own the pipeline. Teams that win keep one person who owns the number and give that person agents. Ads show the same split: see Will AI Replace Performance Marketing?

Is it legal to automate LinkedIn and AI cold calls?

Only with care. Each channel has its own rule. Check these three before you switch an agent on.

  • LinkedIn. LinkedIn does not permit third-party software that automates activity on its site. Members who use it risk having accounts restricted or shut down. Any agent that sends requests or messages for you carries this risk. If automation is off the table, let content build trust: see Best AI Agents for Content Marketing in 2026.
  • AI voice calls in the US. In February 2024, the FCC ruled that AI-generated voices count as artificial voices under the TCPA (the US Telephone Consumer Protection Act). So these calls need the called person's prior express consent, unless an exemption applies. Ask your vendor how it records consent. 11x, for one, describes its outbound calling as consented.
  • Promotional calls in India. In August 2024, TRAI (the telecom regulator) told operators to blacklist unregistered telemarketers and cut their telecom resources for two years, after 1.2 million complaints in 2023. Since the February 2025 amendment, promotional calls must use the 140 number series. A first violation bars outgoing calls for 15 days. Repeat offenders are disconnected for a year and blacklisted.

How do you choose an AI SDR? A 10-point buying checklist

Choose on cost per meeting held, control and risk, not on the demo. Copy this list into your next vendor call.

  1. Write your ICP and offer in one paragraph before the first demo. ICP (ideal customer profile) means who buys, and why now.
  2. Get the full monthly price in writing: platform, seats, credits, mailboxes, domains, LinkedIn seats, dialer.
  3. Do the reply math. Contacts per month × 3.43% = expected replies. If that misses your meeting goal, the plan is too small.
  4. Run copilot mode for 30 days. Approve every message until your edits drop to near zero.
  5. Ask who owns the sending domains and mailboxes. Keep your main company domain out of cold sends.
  6. Ask exactly how it sends on LinkedIn. If it automates your account, accept the restriction risk knowingly, or switch it off.
  7. Check call rules by country. US: prior express consent for AI voices. India: a registered telemarketer on the 140 series.
  8. Test speed to lead. Fill in your own website form at 9 p.m. and time the answer. Under an hour is the bar HBR measured.
  9. Check two-way CRM sync. Every send, reply and meeting must land where your team works.
  10. Judge it on meetings held, not emails sent. Pilot for one quarter at most, with an exit clause, and keep your data if you leave. Ask for a weekly report in money (the kind owners should get).

Questions people ask

Will AI replace SDRs?

Not in 2026. AI SDR agents replace tasks: research, drafts, follow-ups and fast replies. They do not replace the person who picks the market, owns the offer and runs live calls. Gartner predicts agents will outnumber sellers 10 to 1 by 2028, yet fewer than 40% of sellers will say agents improved their productivity.

How much does an AI SDR cost?

As of October 2026, published plans run from $49 a month (Regie.ai Pro) to $2,500 a month (AiSDR Scale). 11x, Artisan, Outreach, Salesloft and Qualified quote on request. Add mailboxes, LinkedIn seats, dialers and data credits to get the real bill.

Is an AI SDR cheaper than a human SDR?

Per month, yes. The Bridge Group puts median SDR on-target pay at $80,000 a year, mostly from North American companies. In India, AmbitionBox shows a typical ₹820,000 to ₹900,000 a year. Compare cost per meeting held, not cost per month.

Is it legal to use AI voice agents for cold calls?

In the US, the FCC ruled in February 2024 that AI voices count as artificial voices under the TCPA, so these calls need prior express consent unless an exemption applies. In India, promotional calls must come from registered telemarketers on the 140 number series.

Can an AI SDR automate LinkedIn safely?

No tool can promise that. LinkedIn does not permit third-party software that automates activity on its site, and accounts that use it can be restricted or shut down. Keep automation off the founder's main profile.

What reply rate should an AI SDR get?

Instantly's 2026 benchmarks put the average cold email reply rate at 3.43%, the top quartile at 5.5% and elite campaigns above 10%. 42% of replies come from follow-ups, so run at least four steps.
